Local ground conditions & planning

Concrete Bases Designed for Maidenhead Properties

Maidenhead properties vary considerably in garden layout and ground conditions. Areas close to the River Thames can experience slightly higher ground moisture levels, which means the base preparation underneath the concrete needs to be carried out properly to avoid movement over time. This is why every installation begins with proper ground preparation before any concrete is poured.

Our installations normally include excavation, a compacted sub-base layer and formwork preparation before the concrete is poured. This ensures the finished slab remains level and stable regardless of seasonal moisture changes in the soil.

What our concrete base installation includes

  • Excavation and removal of loose topsoil
  • Weed membrane installation
  • Compacted hardcore sub-base
  • Edge formwork and level preparation
  • Concrete pour and finishing

Why experience matters

Maidenhead Bases Built Properly — Not Cut Corners

A shed or garden building is only as strong as the base underneath it. If the concrete base is poorly prepared or not properly levelled, the structure above it can start to move, twist or place stress on the frame. Over time this can lead to door misalignment, structural stress and premature wear on the building itself.

With years of installation experience across different property types, we design bases that match the size and weight of the building being installed. From smaller garden sheds to heavier insulated garden offices, the base underneath must support the load properly.
