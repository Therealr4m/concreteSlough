Ground preparation

Why Proper Ground Preparation Matters in High Wycombe

With concrete foundations, the finished concrete is only part of the installation. The ground underneath needs to be prepared properly before the concrete is placed.

A common problem with poorly prepared foundations is movement caused by an inadequate sub-base. If the ground beneath the foundation is not dealt with correctly, the concrete can settle unevenly and the structure above it can be affected.

Our approach is to establish the required footprint first, excavate to the appropriate depth, prepare the ground and compact the supporting material before the concrete is installed. The exact preparation depends on the site and the proposed building rather than using the same arrangement for every property.

For homeowners in High Wycombe, this means the foundation is planned around the actual garden and proposed structure rather than simply pouring concrete onto an existing surface.

Installation process

How We Install Concrete Foundations in High Wycombe

We keep the installation process straightforward and organise the work around the actual site rather than adding unnecessary stages.

1. Confirm the foundation requirements

We establish the proposed footprint, position of the building and access available to the working area. This gives us the information needed to plan the excavation and material delivery.

2. Excavate and prepare the ground

The foundation area is marked out and excavated as required. Loose material is removed and the ground is prepared so the supporting layers can be installed correctly.

3. Prepare the foundation area

The supporting ground and sub-base are compacted and the formwork is positioned to establish the required footprint. This is where much of the important preparation takes place before any concrete arrives.

4. Concrete installation

The concrete is placed into the prepared foundation area and finished to the required level. The installation is checked as the work progresses so the finished foundation is ready for the next stage of construction.

5. Leave the site ready for the next stage

Once the concrete work is complete, the working area is left organised so the intended building or structure can proceed from a properly prepared foundation.
