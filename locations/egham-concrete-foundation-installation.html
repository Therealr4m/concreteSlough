Domestic foundation work

Egham Domestic Concrete Foundations

We install domestic concrete foundations in Egham, carrying out the ground preparation and excavation needed before the concrete is placed. The work is planned around the property, the intended building and the access available to the garden.

A lot of residential properties around Egham have compact outdoor spaces where there is not much room to work. We regularly deal with projects where materials and equipment need to be brought through narrow side access or around existing garden layouts rather than simply driving straight into the working area.

The aim is to prepare the ground properly before the concrete goes down. Poor preparation underneath a foundation can lead to movement or sinking later, which is much harder to correct once the building is already in place.

Where the foundation work matters

  • Domestic outdoor building projects
  • New foundations where an existing base is unsuitable
  • Projects requiring controlled excavation in compact gardens
  • Residential work where restricted access needs to be planned in advance

Local installation approach

Working Around Egham's Residential Property Layouts

Egham has plenty of closely spaced residential streets where gardens and access routes can be fairly tight. On these jobs, access is something we look at before the work starts rather than trying to solve it once excavation is already underway.

Narrow pathways can affect how spoil is removed, how materials reach the garden and how the working area is set up. Where outdoor space is limited, the excavation and preparation need to be controlled so there is enough room to carry out the foundation work without unnecessarily disturbing the surrounding garden.

We also take the position of the proposed building into account from the start. The foundation needs to be prepared where it is actually required, with the surrounding ground and existing garden layout considered as part of the job rather than treated as an afterthought.

Ground preparation

Why Proper Ground Preparation Matters

The concrete itself is only one part of a domestic foundation installation. The ground underneath it needs to be prepared correctly so the finished foundation has a suitable base to sit on.

On Egham properties with compact gardens, excavation needs to be controlled carefully. We remove the required material, prepare the formation and compact the sub-base before moving on to the concrete work.

This is particularly important where a new outdoor building will be used regularly. A foundation that moves underneath the building can create problems with levels, doors and the structure above it. Getting the preparation right at the beginning avoids relying on the concrete to compensate for poor ground conditions.

Our preparation focuses on

  • Preparing the required footprint before concrete placement
  • Controlling excavation where garden space is restricted
  • Creating a properly compacted base beneath the concrete
  • Setting out the foundation accurately for the intended building
Why proper preparation matters

A Straightforward Approach to Domestic Foundation Work

We approach domestic foundation work from the ground up. Before the concrete is placed, we look at the available access, the garden layout and the area being excavated so the installation can be carried out in a controlled way.

That matters on built-up residential streets where working space can be limited. There is less room for unnecessary movement, poor material storage or leaving excavation unfinished, so the job needs to be organised around the property from the beginning.

Our focus is simple: prepare the ground properly, install the foundation where it is required and leave the area ready for the next stage of the building project.

What this means for the homeowner

  • Access requirements are considered before work begins
  • Excavation is planned around the available garden space
  • Ground preparation is completed before the concrete is placed
  • The finished foundation is prepared for the intended building
From access to installation

How We Handle a Concrete Foundation Project in Egham

The first step is understanding the project and how the foundation needs to sit within the garden. Access is checked at this stage, particularly where the property has a narrow side pathway or limited route into the rear garden.

Once the working area is established, the required footprint is marked out and excavation begins. The ground is prepared to the required depth, with the spoil and working space managed around the existing property layout.

The sub-base is then prepared and compacted before the formwork and concrete installation are carried out. Levels and positioning are checked during the process so the finished foundation is ready for the intended structure.
